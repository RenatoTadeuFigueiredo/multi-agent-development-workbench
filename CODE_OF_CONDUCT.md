# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, welcoming, and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and professional respect.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and provider terms.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Help maintain a safe environment for contributors and users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately misleading claims.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing credentials, private source code, or sensitive provider data.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, and other interactions that violate this policy. Responses may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.

Report conduct concerns privately to `renato.tadeu.figueiredo@gmail.com`. Include relevant context and evidence while minimizing personal or sensitive information. Reports will be reviewed promptly, handled as confidentially as practical, and evaluated without retaliation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
